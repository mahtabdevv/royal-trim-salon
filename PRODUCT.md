## Platform

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS (single page, plain JS where needed). Confirmed by the owner.

## Users

Men in Peshawar (and parents bringing boys for a child's cut) looking for a barber or grooming service near Abdara Road. They mostly arrive on a phone, from a Google Maps listing, an Instagram/WhatsApp share, or a search like "salon near Abdara Road". Their job: see what is offered, see the price, and book or walk in.

## Product Purpose

The Royal Trim is a men's salon on Abdara Road, Peshawar, offering haircuts, fades, beard and hair colouring, facials, face treatments, waxing, and keratin / hair treatments. The site exists so a visitor can read the full price list in seconds and book over WhatsApp in one tap. Success is WhatsApp booking messages and calls.

## Positioning

A full-menu men's grooming salon with transparent printed prices across six service families (hair, colour, keratin/treatments, facials, face treatments, waxing) — from a Rs 300 neck round to a Rs 12,000 L'Oréal X-Tenso — under one roof on Abdara Road.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "The Royal Trim". Menus use "The Royal Trim Salon For Men"; the sign says "Hair Salon".
- Logo: gold crown above crossed open scissors, gold serif capitals on black (assets/2.webp is a 3D sign mockup of it).
- Black and gold are the established brand colours.
- Standing preference (owner, 2026-11-01): a simple, conventional business-style website, not a conceptual or themed one. Reference the owner gave: hairandhairsalon.com (another Peshawar men's salon); match its plainness and structure, not its copy or claims.

## Evidence on Hand

- Printed menu flyers (assets/1,3–8.webp) with stock photography; the waxing photo carries an Adobe Stock watermark and must not be reused.
- No real shop photos, testimonials, ratings, staff names, or years-in-business. Do not invent any of these.

## Product Principles

1. Price is the answer: every service shows its rupee price, never "starting from" guesses.
2. One tap to book: WhatsApp is always within reach on a phone.
3. Royal, not stuffy: confident, crafted, and plainly readable for a walk-in customer.
4. Only true claims: no fabricated reviews, awards, or counts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Mobile-first, readable on budget Android phones in bright daylight; English copy.
